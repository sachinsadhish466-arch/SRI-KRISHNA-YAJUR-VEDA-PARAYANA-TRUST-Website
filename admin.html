How to View and Extract Customer Details Directly from Supabase Dashboard

If you prefer viewing or exporting data directly from the official Supabase Cloud Console, follow these 3 simple steps:

  1. Log in to Supabase: Open https://supabase.com/dashboard and select your project.
  2. Open Table Editor: On the left-hand sidebar menu, click on the "Table Editor" icon (looks like a spreadsheet table). Then click on the table named devotee_donations.
  3. View & Extract Data:
    • All customer names, phones, Gothrams, Nakshatrams, Sevas, Gopurams, UPI UTR numbers, and amounts are displayed row-by-row in real-time.
    • To extract to Excel/CSV: Click the "Export" or "Download CSV" button located at the top-right corner of the table. Your complete customer spreadsheet will immediately download to your computer!
