Visual Splendour of Thillai Kshetram

Chidambaram Temples Pictures (சிதம்பரம் திருக்கோயில் புகைப்படங்கள்)

Immerse yourself in the divine grace of Lord Nataraja, the golden Kanaka Sabha roof, ancient Chola stone towers, and daily Vedic chanting sessions.

Sanctum Sanctorum

Lord Thillai Nataraja

The golden icon of Lord Nataraja performing the eternal cosmic dance within the halo of cosmic fire (Prabha Mandala), adorned with divine garlands and lamps.

Architecture

Kanaka Sabha (Golden Roof)

The world-famous golden roof of Kanaka Sabha with 21,600 pure gold tiles, 72,000 gold nails, and 9 radiant golden spires (Kalashas).

Holy Tank & Tower

Raja Gopuram & Sivaganga Tank

The monumental Dravidian gateway tower reflecting in the placid waters of the Sivaganga Theertham during morning sunlight.

Trust Activities

Krishna Yajur Veda Parayana

Venerable Vedic pundits seated in the stone mandapam reciting ancient Taittiriya Samhita hymns before Lord Nataraja.

Festivals & Culture

Natyanjali Dance Offering

Classical Bharatanatyam dancer rendering devotion in the 1000-pillar hall during the annual Maha Shivaratri Natyanjali celebration.

Divine Seal

Trust Emblem & Seal

Sacred gold crest depicting Lord Nataraja, the Holy Om, Vedic scriptures, and the Sanskrit motto 'Vedo Akhilo Dharmamoolam'.
