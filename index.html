Sacred Marvel of South India

Speciality of Chidambaram Kshetram

Chidambaram Thillai Nataraja Temple is one of the most sacred pilgrimage shrines on Earth, embodying the ether (Akasa) element among the Pancha Bhoota Sthalams.

Space Element

Akasa Sthalam (Formless Space)

Among the 5 elemental Shiva temples (Kalahasti - Air, Thiruvannamalai - Fire, Thiruvanaikaval - Water, Kanchipuram - Earth), Chidambaram alone represents the limitless Ether/Space. Here Shiva is worshipped in three forms: Form (Sthula - Nataraja), Formless (Arupa - Chidambara Rahasyam), and Form-Formless (Rupa-Arupa - Sphatika Lingam).

  • Complete Cosmic Geometry & Center of Earth's magnetic equator
  • Lord Nataraja in eternal Ananda Tandava state
Read Full Details
Divine Mystery

Chidambara Rahasyam (The Secret)

To the right of Lord Nataraja hangs a velvet curtain representing Maya (illusion). When drawn aside during Deeparadhana, it reveals an empty space adorned only with a garland of golden Vilva leaves. This signifies that God is all-pervading space without form or beginning.

  • Golden Bilva garland (Swarna Vilva Mala)
  • Liberation through realization of the infinite Void

Sacred Architecture

Kanaka Sabha & 5 Divine Sabhas

The temple houses five halls: Chit Sabha, Kanaka Sabha, Nritta Sabha, Deva Sabha, and Raja Sabha. The golden roof of Kanaka Sabha is covered with 21,600 gold plates symbolizing the human breaths per day, fastened with 72,000 gold nails representing the subtle nadis.

  • 9 Golden Kalashas representing Navashaktis
  • 108 Natya Shastra postures carved on Raja Gopurams

Sacred Mission of Sanatana Dharma

Sri Krishna Yajur Veda Parayana Trust

Founded under the divine blessings of Lord Thillai Nataraja, our trust is dedicated to preserving the pristine chanting and oral heritage of the Krishna Yajur Veda (Taittiriya Shakha) at the sanctum sanctorum of Chidambaram.

Every single day, renowned Vedic scholars and Agnihotris chant the sacred Samhita, Pada, Krama, and Ghana pathas to generate divine vibrations that invoke global peace, seasonal rainfall, and spiritual illumination for all humanity.

Major Festivals & Function Days

Plan your pilgrimage to Chidambaram during the auspicious function days when Lord Nataraja emerges for the grand Maha Abhishekam.

Margazhi Month (Dec - Jan)

Arudhra Darisanam (Thiruvathirai)

மார்கழி திருவாதிரை ஆருத்ரா தரிசனம்

The paramount annual festival of Chidambaram. A 10-day celebration culminating in the grand dawn Abhishekam of Lord Nataraja and Goddess Sivakami with sacred waters, milk, honey, sandal paste, and the traditional sweet offering 'Thiruvathirai Kali'.

10-Day Brahmotsavam View Details
Aani Month (June - July)

Aani Thirumanjanam

ஆனி திருமஞ்சனம் பெருவிழா

The second major Brahmotsavam where Lord Nataraja and Goddess Sivakami are brought to the Raja Sabha (1000-pillar hall) for public Maha Abhishekam. Witnessed by hundreds of thousands of ecstatic devotees.

Uttara Nakshatram View Details
Maha Shivaratri (Feb - Mar)

Natyanjali Dance Festival

நாட்டியாஞ்சலி நடன விழா

A 5-day national festival where the finest classical Bharatanatyam, Kuchipudi, Kathak, and Mohiniyattam exponents from across the world offer their art at the feet of the Lord of Dance, Nataraja.

Chidambaram Nataraja Temple Routine

Daily Aru Kaala Pooja & Darshan Timings

Lord Nataraja is worshipped six times a day through the traditional Vedic rites by the venerable Podu Dikshitars.

Kaalam / Pooja Name Timings Significance & Seva Performed Special Highlight
1. Kala Santhi (Early Morning) 06:00 AM - 07:30 AM Morning awakening, Go Pooja (Cow worship), and preliminary aradhana. Crystal Lingam (Sphatika Lingam) Abhishekam
2. Irandam Kalam (Second Morning) 08:30 AM - 09:30 AM Second worship with sacred food offerings and herbal concoctions. Krishna Yajur Veda Parayana Chanting
3. Uchi Kalam (Noon Pooja) 11:30 AM - 12:30 PM Midday grand Abhishekam to the Ratnasabhapati (Ruby Nataraja). Ruby Nataraja (Ratnasabhapati) Deeparadhana
Temple Closed Afternoon 12:30 PM - 04:30 PM Sanctum gates closed for afternoon rest. Prakara open for pradakshina
4. Sayaratchai (Evening Sunset) 05:30 PM - 06:30 PM Sunset lamps lighting, Veda Parayanam, grand Karpoora Harathi. Chidambara Rahasyam Darshan curtain opened
5. Irandam Kalam (Night Pooja) 07:30 PM - 08:30 PM Night aradhana with fragrant flower garlands and neivedhyam. Evening Veda Ghoshams
6. Ardha Jamam (Midnight Pooja) 09:30 PM - 10:00 PM All powers of Shiva across the universe converge into Nataraja's Padukas for the night. Paduka Procession to Palli Arai (Holy Bed Chamber)
