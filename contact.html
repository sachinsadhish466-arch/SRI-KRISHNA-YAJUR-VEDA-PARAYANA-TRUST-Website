Pilgrim Travel Information

How to Reach Chidambaram (வழித்தடங்கள்)

By Train (Railway)

Chidambaram Railway Station (Station Code: CDM) is situated just 1.5 KM from the Nataraja Temple. Regular express trains connect Chidambaram with Chennai Egmore, Tiruchirappalli, Madurai, Kumbakonam, Thanjavur, Rameswaram, and Tirupati.

Autos & Battery Rickshaws available 24/7 at the station.

By Road (Buses)

Chidambaram is well connected via NH 32 (East Coast Road) and NH 36. Frequent direct government (SETC / TNSTC) and luxury private AC buses operate from Chennai (235 KM, ~5 hrs), Pondicherry (65 KM, ~1.5 hrs), Trichy (130 KM), and Kumbakonam (70 KM).

By Air (Flights)

Nearest Airports:
• Tiruchirappalli International Airport (TRZ): 135 KM (~3 hrs drive)
• Puducherry Airport (PNY): 70 KM (~1.5 hrs drive)
• Chennai International Airport (MAA): 220 KM (~4.5 hrs drive)

Nearby Sacred Temples to Visit Along with Chidambaram

1. Thillai Kali Temple: 1.5 KM from Nataraja Temple (Goddess Kali shrine).
2. Sirkazhi Sattanathar: 20 KM (Birthplace of Tirugnanasambandar).
3. Vaitheeswaran Koil: 25 KM (Lord Shiva as the Supreme Healer).
4. Bhuvanagiri: 10 KM (Avatar Sthala of Sri Raghavendra Swamy).
