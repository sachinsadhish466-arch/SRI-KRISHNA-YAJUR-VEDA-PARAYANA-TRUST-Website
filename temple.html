Pancha Bhoota Akasa Kshetram

The Heart Center of the Universe (Daharākāśa)

Chidambaram (literally Chit = Consciousness, Ambaram = Sky / Space) represents the supreme sky of pure consciousness. According to ancient Vedic cosmology, this sacred spot is the center of the earth's magnetic equator and corresponds to the heart lotus (Anahata Chakra) of the cosmic Virat Purusha.

Here, Lord Shiva danced the cosmic dance of bliss — Ananda Tandavam — to grant Darshan to the great sages Patanjali (the incarnation of Adisesha) and Vyaghrapada (the tiger-footed rishi).

"Tiruvarur for Birth, Kashi for Death, Thiruvannamalai for Remembrance, and Chidambaram for Mere Darshan grants Liberation (Mukti)!"

The Greatest Secret of Sanatana Dharma

Chidambara Rahasyam (The Divine Secret)

In the sanctum next to Lord Nataraja is the Chidambara Rahasyam. A curtain made of rich velvet is drawn across this chamber, symbolizing the veil of Maya (ignorance and illusion) that conceals the absolute truth from the human mind.

When the Dikshitar priest pulls aside the curtain during the sacred arati, devotees behold an empty space. There is no idol, stone, or physical representation. Hanging in this void is a magnificent garland of Golden Vilva leaves (Swarna Vilva Mala).

The secret is that the Supreme Godhead is not bound by form, attributes, or names — Shiva is the boundless, eternal, luminous Space of Consciousness (Chidakasha). Realizing this truth within oneself grants immediate liberation.

Divine Numerology in Sacred Architecture

Kanaka Sabha (The Golden Roof)

Built by the illustrious Chola monarchs, the golden roof (Porkoorai) of Kanaka Sabha is a physical representation of the human body and the cosmic anatomy.

21,600 Golden Tiles

The golden roof is clad with exactly 21,600 gold plates. This precisely represents the 21,600 respirations (breaths) that a human being takes every 24 hours (Soham mantra).

72,000 Golden Nails

The gold plates are fastened with exactly 72,000 gold nails. According to Yogic science, this matches the 72,000 energy channels (Nadis) circulating through the human nervous system.

9 Golden Kalashas

Crowning the ridge of the roof are 9 pure gold Kalashas (vessels), symbolizing the nine manifestations of Shakti (Navashaktis) presiding over the universe.

Five Cosmic Stages of Shiva

The Five Sacred Sabhas

1. Chit Sabha (Sanctum)

The holiest innermost sanctum where Lord Nataraja and Sivakami Sundari reside, alongside the Chidambara Rahasyam.

2. Kanaka Sabha (Golden Hall)

The hall right in front of Chit Sabha with the golden roof where daily Aru Kaala poojas and Veda Parayanam are offered.

3. Nritta Sabha (Hall of Dance)

Carved like a majestic chariot drawn by horses with 56 intricately sculpted stone pillars, commemorating the dance duel with Kali.

4. Deva Sabha (Hall of Deities)

The spacious hall where the Utsava deities and Panchamurtis are kept and special community meetings are convened.

5. Raja Sabha (1000-Pillar Hall)

The monumental Thousand Pillar Hall measuring 340 ft by 190 ft, where the grand Maha Abhishekam of Arudhra and Aani is witnessed.

Sri Govindaraja Perumal Shrine

One of the 108 Divya Desams located inside the Nataraja temple courtyard, symbolizing the eternal unity of Hari and Hara.

The Podu Dikshitars (Thillai Muvayiravar - The 3,000 Priests)

The hereditary custodians of the Chidambaram Nataraja Temple are the Podu Dikshitars, also known as Tillai Vaazh Andanar. They were brought from Mount Kailasa by Lord Shiva Himself. When counted, only 2,999 were present — Lord Nataraja then declared, "I am the 3,000th Dikshitar amongst you!"

The Dikshitars follow the unique Vedic ritual manual created by sage Patanjali. The Sri Krishna Yajur Veda Parayana Trust works in close coordination with the learned Dikshitars to conduct daily Veda recitation at Kanaka Sabha.
